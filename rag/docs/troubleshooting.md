# Troubleshooting

## Booking Doesn't Show in My Account
Bookings made as a guest (without signing in) are not automatically linked to a new account. To link them:
1. Sign in to your account
2. Go to "My Bookings" → "Link existing booking"
3. Enter the booking confirmation number and email used at booking
4. Click "Link" — the booking appears immediately

## Confirmation Email Not Received
1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Verify the email address on your booking is correct
3. Wait up to 15 minutes — emails are queued during peak hours
4. Request a resend from "My Bookings" → "Resend confirmation"
5. If still nothing after 30 minutes, contact support@hotel.example with your booking code

## Wrong Dates on My Booking
You can modify dates from "My Bookings" → "Modify" if your rate plan allows it. Date changes are subject to:
- Availability at the new dates
- Any rate difference between original and new dates
- A modification fee of $20 per booking (waived for Gold/Platinum members)

Non-refundable bookings cannot be modified.

## App or Website Not Working
1. Refresh the page or restart the app
2. Clear browser cache and cookies (or update the app)
3. Try a different browser or device
4. Check status.hotel.example for known incidents
