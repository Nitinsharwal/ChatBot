# Cancellation Policy

## Free Cancellation Window
All Standard and Deluxe room bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to **24 hours before the scheduled check-in time** (local hotel time). A full refund is issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Non-Refundable Rates
Bookings made under the "Non-Refundable" rate plan cannot be cancelled or refunded. These bookings are clearly marked at the time of reservation and are typically 15–20% cheaper than flexible rates.

## Late Cancellation
Cancellations made less than 24 hours before check-in will be charged one (1) night's room rate. The remainder of the booking is refunded.

## No-Show Policy
If the guest does not arrive by 11:59 PM on the check-in date and no cancellation has been made, the entire booking is forfeited and no refund is issued.

## How to Cancel
1. Sign in to your account
2. Go to "My Bookings"
3. Click the booking and select "Cancel"
4. Confirm the cancellation reason

You will receive an email confirming the cancellation within 5 minutes.
