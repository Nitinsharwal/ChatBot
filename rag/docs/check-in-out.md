# Check-in & Check-out

## Standard Times
- **Check-in:** from 3:00 PM (15:00) local time
- **Check-out:** by 11:00 AM (11:00) local time

## Early Check-in
Early check-in is available based on room availability and cannot be guaranteed in advance. If you request early check-in:
- Before 10:00 AM: charged 50% of one night's rate
- Between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM: complimentary if rooms are available

## Late Check-out
- Until 1:00 PM: complimentary, subject to availability
- Until 6:00 PM: 50% of one night's rate
- After 6:00 PM: full night's charge

## Required at Check-in
- Government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, national ID)
- The credit card used to make the booking
- The booking confirmation number (or email confirmation)

## Mobile Key
Guests with a confirmed booking can activate a mobile room key in the hotel app 24 hours before check-in. This allows you to skip the front desk and go directly to your room.
