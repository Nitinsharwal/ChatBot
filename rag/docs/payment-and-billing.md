# Payment & Billing

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- Digital wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal
- UPI (India): Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm
- Bank transfer (corporate bookings only, requires 48-hour processing time)

## When You Are Charged
- **Flexible rate:** card is authorized at booking, charged at check-in
- **Non-refundable rate:** card is charged in full immediately at booking
- **Pay-at-hotel rate:** card is verified at booking, charged at check-out

## Currency
All prices are displayed in the currency you select at booking. The hotel may charge in local currency; your bank's exchange rate applies.

## Receipts and Invoices
A receipt is automatically emailed at check-out. For a GST/VAT invoice, contact the hotel front desk during your stay or email billing@hotel.example within 30 days.

## Payment Failed
If your payment fails:
1. Verify the card is not expired and has sufficient funds
2. Check that your billing address matches the card
3. Try a different card or payment method
4. If the issue persists, contact your bank to authorize the transaction

If a charge appears on your statement but the booking shows as failed, contact support — this is usually a pending authorization that releases within 5–10 business days.

## Double Charge
If you see two charges for the same booking, one is almost always a pending authorization that will drop off. If both charges settle after 10 business days, email support@hotel.example with both transaction IDs for a refund within 5–7 business days.
