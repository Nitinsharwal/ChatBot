# Rooms & Amenities

## Room Types
| Type | Size | Bed | Max Guests | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 24 m² | 1 Queen | 2 | WiFi, TV, Tea/Coffee |
| Deluxe | 32 m² | 1 King | 2 | + City view, Bathrobe, Minibar |
| Family | 40 m² | 2 Queen | 4 | + Sofa bed, Extra bathroom |
| Suite | 55 m² | 1 King + Living room | 3 | + Lounge access, Espresso machine |

## Included in All Rooms
- Free high-speed WiFi (up to 100 Mbps)
- 50" smart TV with streaming apps
- Air conditioning and heating
- In-room safe (fits a 15" laptop)
- Daily housekeeping

## Hotel Facilities
- 24-hour reception and concierge
- Fitness centre (open 5:00 AM – 11:00 PM)
- Outdoor pool (seasonal, May–September)
- Restaurant and bar (breakfast 6:30–10:30 AM, dinner 6:00–10:30 PM)
- Business centre and meeting rooms

## Parking
On-site parking is available at $25 per night for guests. Valet service is $40 per night. Subject to availability — reserve in advance via the booking portal.

## Pet Policy
Small pets under 15 kg are welcome in Standard and Deluxe rooms for a $30 per night cleaning fee. Maximum 1 pet per room. Service animals stay free of charge with valid documentation.

## Smoking
All rooms and indoor areas are strictly non-smoking. A cleaning fee of $250 is charged for any smoking detected in a room. Designated outdoor smoking areas are provided.
