# Account & Loyalty

## Creating an Account
Sign up with your email or via Google/Apple Sign-In. Account creation is free and gives you:
- Faster checkout (saved payment methods and preferences)
- Booking history in one place
- Loyalty points on every stay
- Exclusive member-only rates (up to 10% off)

## Password Reset
1. Go to the sign-in page and click "Forgot password"
2. Enter the email on your account
3. Check your inbox for a reset link (valid for 30 minutes)
4. If you don't see the email, check spam, then contact support

## Loyalty Program — StayRewards
- Earn 10 points per $1 spent on rooms
- 1,000 points = $10 redemption credit
- Tiers: Silver (0 pts), Gold (10,000 pts/year), Platinum (40,000 pts/year)
- Gold and above: free room upgrades when available, late checkout to 2 PM
- Platinum: complimentary breakfast, welcome amenity, suite upgrades

Points expire 24 months after the last qualifying stay.

## Updating Your Profile
Sign in → Profile → Edit. You can update your name, email, phone, payment methods, and communication preferences. Email changes require verification via a link sent to the new address.

## Deleting Your Account
Email privacy@hotel.example with the subject "Delete my account". We process deletion within 30 days. Active or upcoming bookings must be completed or cancelled first.
